## Real-music Product Probe

Two materially different user-rights-attested local tracks were used. Private/local media was not committed.

Observed canonical decision evidence:

- Track A: top-window score `0.9460`, source range `6.05–12.05s`;
- Track B: top-window score `0.9553`, source range `29.65–35.65s`;
- core selection chose Track B even when cross-track candidate input was deliberately reversed;
- ordinary Track B moment: `30.45–36.45s`;
- selected Track B moment: `29.65–35.65s`;
- source-audio policy: `MUTE`;
- all six candidate/moment/mix previews consumed canonical decisions;
- Product Probe gates: `9/9 PASS`;
- rendered-output QC detected no clipping or accidental silence;
- source music SHA-256 values remained unchanged.

Track B BeatMap confidence was low at `0.0633`. This value remained visible; no hidden score compensation was introduced.

## Human Gate

The user listened to the real Product Probe comparisons and explicitly chose:

- music candidate: **Track B**;
- music moment: **selected**;
- mix: **structured**;
- obvious audible defect: **none**.

This Human Gate accepts the product result despite the low BeatMap confidence because the preferred audible result matched the selected candidate/moment/mix and no blocking quality defect was identified.

## Invariants confirmed at closure

- local/user-rights-attested music remains the commercial-safe baseline;
- decision truth equals execution truth;
- source video/audio Assets are not destructively modified;
- source-audio preserve/mute policy is explicit and executable;
- music selection is deterministic and caller-order independent;
- BeatMap/music evidence describes soundtrack timing but does not own the video timeline;
- Renderer does not recreate editorial decisions;
- no synthetic music substitutes for Product Probe evidence.

## Known limitations carried forward

- BeatMap quality/confidence still requires broader corpus benchmarking;
- source-audio `DUCK` semantics are intentionally fail-closed until explicitly owned;
- richer SFX/voiceover workflows and final EDL time-varying audio productization continue downstream;
- proxy/cache productization remains R0.12 scope.

These are not R0.10 closure blockers.
