## Capability semantics proven

The accepted implementation distinguishes:

- valid transcript/subtitle execution;
- `NO_SPEECH`;
- subtitle `SKIPPED` when no grounded speech evidence exists and ASR is unavailable;
- capability-unavailable failure when grounded speech requires unavailable ASR;
- synthetic voice remains a typed future seam and is not exposed as an ordinary-user success path without an approved backend.
