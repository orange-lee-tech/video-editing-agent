The production Editing entry now exists above the previously accepted R0.9 grounded retrieval/resolution kernel.

Editing-only can use:

`exact persisted Brief + persisted eligible local Shot/ShotAnalysis evidence`
`→ provider-neutral Director proposal`
`→ production Director workflow`
`→ persisted revisioned EditPlan`

without fabricating ScriptPlan or ShootingPlan.

- Resolver, CandidateWindow implementation, retrieval algorithms, EDLBuilder, Renderer and other STOP-scope production systems were not materially redesigned.
