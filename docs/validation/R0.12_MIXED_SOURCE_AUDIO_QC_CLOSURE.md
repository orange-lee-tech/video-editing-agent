## Accepted behavior

The implementation closes the whole-EditPlan source-audio granularity defect without moving Resolver or Renderer authority.

Accepted semantics:

- `SourceAudioTreatment` targets one grounded `ResolvedSelection` by `selection_id` and its exact selected source range;
- explicit per-selection treatment overrides the legacy whole-EditPlan `SourceAudioPolicy` compatibility default;
- source treatment supports `PRESERVE`, `DUCK`, and `MUTE` independently across mixed selected ranges;
- exact source range equality is checked against the already assembled grounded selection; Audio Editorial does not derive source timestamps;
- `DUCK` maps deterministically into canonical EDL `GAIN + DUCK` automation on the targeted SOURCE_AUDIO segment;
- unknown, duplicate/conflicting, and range-mismatched treatments fail closed;
- VoiceTreatment supports `PRESERVE`, `CLEAN`, `ALLOW_REVOICE`, and `DO_NOT_USE_ORIGINAL`;
- required speech cannot be silently removed by `PRESERVE`/`CLEAN`, and `ALLOW_REVOICE` does not fabricate an unapproved replacement lane;
- ordinary non-required-speech material may be explicitly `MUTE` without a fake voice-policy declaration;
- `DO_NOT_USE_ORIGINAL` remains consistent with explicit source mute;
- a typed structural audible-lane QC rejects non-silent intent when canonical EDL contains no approved audible segment;
- intentional silence remains valid;
- fully muted audio does not satisfy the audible-lane gate;
- PCM peak/RMS/silent-fraction inspection remains separate execution evidence.

## Authority review

### Resolver

Unchanged. Resolver / `ResolvedSelection` still owns the selected Shot and exact source range.

### Audio Editorial

Owns treatment intent only. It may reference a grounded selection/range but cannot choose footage or invent time coordinates.

### EDLBuilder

Owns deterministic translation of approved treatment into exact canonical SOURCE_AUDIO segments/automation.

### Renderer

Unchanged by the implementation and acceptance repair. Renderer remains execution-only and does not infer, select, or repair audio treatment.

### Canonical EDL

Remains sole executable timeline authority.

## Acceptance repair

ChatGPT acceptance review of `ac5eb16fc8ecfb5ed29306826942765d264e0f3d` found one narrow semantic defect: the default `VoiceTreatment.PRESERVE` caused a non-speech explicit `MUTE` selection to fail even when `required_speech=False`.

Repair `ffb5dbd7d3fc4e995f89a7a231910fa0295fcbba` limits the `PRESERVE/CLEAN + MUTE` speech-protection conflict to `required_speech=True` and adds a direct two-clip regression:

- selection A explicit PRESERVE;
- selection B ordinary non-speech explicit MUTE;
- assembly succeeds;
- SOURCE_AUDIO contains only selection A.

Required-speech PRESERVE, CLEAN, and ALLOW_REVOICE fail-closed behavior remains covered.

## Remaining limitations

- `CLEAN` is currently a typed permission/semantic protection policy, not a new DSP denoiser;
- `ALLOW_REVOICE` is permission only and creates no voice asset without a separately approved replacement;
- explicit treatment currently requires exact equality with the full grounded selected source range rather than implicit subrange splitting;
- audible-lane QC is structural/intention-aware; final execution-level PCM diagnostics remain separate.

These limitations are explicit and do not block this Work Order.
