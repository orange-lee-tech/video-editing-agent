## Shared Commercial Authority quality guard

The stabilization work exposed a systemic failure mode: narrative/marketing intent and concrete factual authority can be interpreted differently by independent model calls unless the authority model is explicit and shared.

The retained invariant is:

> `objective`, `audience`, and positioning/core-message intent may define narrative context, but they do not establish concrete product property, performance, fit, adequacy, operability, material, reliability, or outcome facts.

Concrete claims and successful demonstrations require authoritative support.

When an authoritative fact establishes only a visible mechanism, action, or state, planning output must remain a neutral observable description of that mechanism/action/state. Ease, convenience, simplicity, sufficiency, fit, performance, reliability, or resulting benefit require separate support.

Reviewer diagnostics are non-authoritative. Reviewer authority remains accept/veto only; Domain owners remain responsible for commit.
