# R0.7B Product Planning Closure

**Status:** CLOSED WITH HUMAN ACCEPTANCE

**Snapshot:** 2026-08-13

## Scope

R0.7B validates the pre-production planning chain:

```text
Brief
↓
ScriptPlan
↓
ShootingPlan
```

The goal was not final video rendering. The goal was to prove that a user can obtain a useful, controllable, factually grounded production plan before shooting.

## Product Probe evidence

The final Product Probe evaluated two realistic cases:

1. Product Advertisement
2. Natural Vlog

Both cases reached human evaluation readiness after the shared commercial authority recovery.

## Human acceptance

Accepted dimensions:

- usefulness;
- shooting executability;
- factual fidelity;
- expected coverage.

## Quality notes

Product Advertisement estimated duration was shorter than the Brief target (approximately 23 seconds versus 30 seconds).

This is recorded as a non-blocking quality note. The plan remains usable because real shooting and editing contain natural pacing space, and the phase acceptance criteria require usable ScriptPlan/ShootingPlan control rather than exact duration equality.

## Durable engineering conclusions

The phase confirmed:

- LLM output remains proposal data, not domain authority;
- commercial facts require explicit authority support;
- positioning intent cannot silently become factual claims;
- semantic review should veto unsupported proposals rather than rewrite creative content;
- shooting demonstrations may themselves imply factual claims and require the same authority discipline.
