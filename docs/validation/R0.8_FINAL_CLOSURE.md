# R0.8 Final Closure

**Status:** CLOSED

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Corpus and environment

The probe used seven anonymous, gitignored real captured product clips (approximately 16.9 MiB)
tracked by `tests/fixtures/media/r0_8h/corpus_manifest.json`. Coverage included handheld product
demonstration, camera motion, hand/product interaction, low motion, and weak/blurred footage.

CPU-only local runtimes were reused: FFmpeg 8.1, OpenCV 4.13.0.92, faster-whisper 1.2.1, Silero
VAD 6.2.1/ONNX Runtime, and sentence-transformers 5.6.0 with multilingual E5 revision
`614241f622f53c4eeff9890bdc4f31cfecc418b3`. No runtime/model download was required.

## Limitations

Speech was deterministic local TTS mixed with captured ambient audio. Natural spontaneous human
speech, microphone speech acoustics, lip sync, and talking-head visual behavior were not validated.
R0.8 does not claim those capabilities. This small corpus is a closure baseline, not a broad
accuracy benchmark.
