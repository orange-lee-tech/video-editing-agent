3. Service Description

3.1 Youqi (Video Editing Agent) is an AI-assisted desktop video creation and editing tool. Its core functions include using local logic and optional AI models to assist with script generation, shooting planning, editing plans, and related video-editing operations. The Software runs as a local EXE desktop application and does not require registration of a project-operated account.

3.2 The Software is not a general text-to-video or automatic network-stock generator. AI-assisted functions primarily operate on user-provided materials and instructions to assist with understanding, planning, editing, arrangement, and script suggestions. Final visual media is primarily derived from local media selected by the user.
